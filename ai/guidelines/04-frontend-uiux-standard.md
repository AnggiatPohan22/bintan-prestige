# Frontend UI/UX Standard Guideline

## Main Goal
Keep the public Bintan Prestige website premium, fast, readable, mobile-first, and controlled by backend CMS data.

## Required References
- Master rule: `AGENTS.md`
- Related skills: `frontend-skill.md`, `uiux-skill.md`, `design-system-skill.md`, `component-library-skill.md`
- Related guidelines: `03-backend-data-processing.md`, `07-seo-ai-discovery.md`, `08-performance-optimization.md`

## Rules
- Use TailwindCSS and Blade components/partials.
- Keep critical content visible in HTML.
- Render only backend-prepared data in Blade.
- Use semantic HTML and clear H1/H2 structure.
- Use mobile-first responsive layouts.
- Use optimized images with meaningful alt text.
- Keep animations lightweight and transform/opacity based.
- Avoid heavy JavaScript and frontend business logic.
- Avoid generic AI-looking sections.

## Bintan Prestige Style
- Luxury travel
- Clean and modern
- Premium spacing
- Strong visual hierarchy
- Travel-focused imagery
- Consistent buttons, cards, badges, CTAs, breadcrumbs, and section headings

## Safety
- Do not remove public sections without approval.
- Do not hardcode content that already exists in CMS data.
- Do not rebuild the frontend from zero unless explicitly requested.
- Check SEO, performance, and responsive behavior before reporting completion.
