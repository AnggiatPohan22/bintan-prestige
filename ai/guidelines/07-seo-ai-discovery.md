# SEO & AI Discovery Guideline

## Main Goal
Make public pages discoverable by search engines and AI answer systems while avoiding spam, thin pages, and hidden critical content.

## Required References
- Master rule: `AGENTS.md`
- Related skill: `seo-ai-discovery-skill.md`
- Related guidelines: `03-backend-data-processing.md`, `04-frontend-uiux-standard.md`, `08-performance-optimization.md`

## Public Page Requirements
- Meta title
- Meta description
- Canonical URL
- Open Graph data
- Twitter/X card data
- Robots meta
- Sitemap inclusion
- Clean slug
- Breadcrumb
- Logical H1/H2 structure
- Image alt text
- Internal links
- JSON-LD schema where relevant

## AI Discovery Rules
- Important content must be present in server-rendered HTML.
- FAQ answers should be concise and visible.
- Destination and product pages should include useful, specific travel content.
- Business credibility signals should be visible.
- `robots.txt` and `sitemap.xml` must not expose admin/private areas.
- Do not generate mass low-quality AI pages.
- Do not create fake reviews, fake mentions, or misleading authority signals.
