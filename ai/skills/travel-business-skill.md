# Travel Business Skill

## Main Goal
Understand Bintan Prestige as a travel/taxi/tour/activity CMS.

## Business Modules
- Taxi
- Hotel
- Activity
- Tour Package
- Destinations
- Blog
- FAQ
- Contact
- WhatsApp booking

## Rules
- Content should support visitor trust.
- Product detail must answer visitor questions.
- CTA should guide user to WhatsApp.
- Destination pages should support SEO.
- Price display must be clear.
- Duration, meeting point, itinerary, notes, and FAQs must be easy to read.
